The source depicts Sam Bird pulling away in a Williams-Cosworth FW32 at
the Goodwood Festival of Speed on 3 July 2010.

Changes: excerpts at 4.2s, 13.9s and 18.2s were converted to mono 48 kHz,
filtered, level-normalized, and crossfaded into seamless idle/mid/high
engine loops. OGG and MP3 variants are provided. The adapted recordings
remain licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. No endorsement is implied.

Only these recorded audio assets are covered by this attribution;
the project's procedural effects and source code are separate works.
