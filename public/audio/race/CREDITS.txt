Changes: a steady 2.5-second section beginning at 8.35 seconds was converted
into mono 48 kHz idle and running loops. Filtering, envelope stabilization,
100 ms loop crossfades and click-free PCM endpoints were applied. The
running adaptation is pitch-shifted by 1.65 for the classic high-rev tone.
Both WAV adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 3.0. No endorsement is implied.

The legacy f1-idle/mid/high OGG and MP3 assets are unused. They remain
adaptations of Williams-Cosworth FW32 (2010), by the same author under
CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Williams-Cosworth_FW32_(2010).ogg

Only the recordings are covered by these credits. Procedural effects and
source code are separate works.
